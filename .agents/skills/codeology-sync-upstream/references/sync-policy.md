# Upstream intake policy

- `upstream/main` is a source, not an automatically trusted release channel.
- Keep imported curriculum under its stable upstream paths.
- Keep original Codeology content under `content/codeology/`.
- Keep adaptations under `content/overrides/<source-id>/`.
- Record source ID, canonical path, upstream commit SHA, licence and adaptation status.
- Discovery and labelling may be automated; merge and publication require review.
- A rename or removal must produce an explicit migration report.
- Never resolve a conflict by discarding Codeology work or silently rewriting upstream attribution.
